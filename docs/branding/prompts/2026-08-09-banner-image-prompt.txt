Create a single ultra-wide cinematic banner image.

STYLE AND LIGHT
Photorealistic, richly detailed cinematic render. Lit like a Gustave Doré
engraving translated into film: deep storm chiaroscuro, one dramatic
backlight source, heavy atmosphere, rain-charged air. Serious and
monumental, never whimsical.

SETTING
Night on the slope of Mount Helicon under a breaking storm. The location is
at once an ancient Greek sanctuary and a concert stage — the same structure
serving both purposes, not one decorating the other.

REAR CENTRE — MNEME
A colossal bronze cult statue of a woman, deeply oxidized to green verdigris,
scaled so large she reads as architecture rather than as a person. She holds
no instrument and is not performing. Water pours continuously from her cupped
hands, falls down the mountainside, and runs in a stone channel across the
stage below. A break in the storm backlights her.

THE STAGE
A long stage built from a wall of stacked amplifier cabinets, their grille
cloth reading as weathered stone, their corners shod in bronze, stacked like
ashlar masonry. A winged horse — Pegasus — is carved in relief into the face
of the cabinets. Thick cables run across the stage floor like vines and roots.

STAGE LEFT — MELETE
A woman in a chiton with a studded leather belt and a laurel wreath, standing
on a stone plinth, caught mid-practice and in motion. She is working, not
performing: brow furrowed, mouth moving as she counts. In front of her a music
stand holds a printed sheet showing both five-line staff notation and six-line
tablature. Beside her on the cabinet sit a mechanical pyramid metronome in
bright polished bronze, and a wax writing tablet with a bronze stylus.

STAGE RIGHT — AOEDE
A woman mirrored to Melete at exactly the same scale, the same eye level, and
the same prominence, on an identical stone plinth. She is singing — head back,
mouth open, mid-note. She holds nothing; one hand is raised in the mano
cornuta, index and little fingers extended. Behind her stand racks of rolled
scrolls and handwritten set lists.

Melete and Aoede must read as equals: identical size, identical height,
symmetrically balanced left and right of the colossus.

INSTRUMENTS
On stands across the stage, spanning 2,500 years, with no single instrument
favoured: an ancient Greek kithara, an aulos (double reed pipe), a
hurdy-gurdy, a Flying V electric guitar, an electric bass, and a theremin,
plus a drum kit whose shells are bronze gone green. Place the kithara and the
Flying V side by side so their silhouettes echo one another.

THE WINGS — BRONZE AUTOMATA
Several small bronze automata attend the rig at both wings: coiling cable,
holding a sheet of music up to the light, adjusting a microphone stand. They
are the smallest figures in the frame, unmistakably attendants, and none of
them touches an instrument or resembles one of the women.

MATERIAL RULE
Every bronze surface that is handled daily is bright and polished — the
metronome, the automata's hands, the fretboards, the rim of the spring. Every
bronze surface that is static or neglected is oxidized deep green.

TEXT (render cleanly, spell exactly)
- Cut in Roman capitals into the three stone plinths: MNEME beneath the
  colossus, MELETE beneath the left figure, AOEDE beneath the right figure.
- Across the front amplifier grille, in heavy blackletter, positioned like a
  band logo: MNEMOSYS
- On a weathered sign in the upper right of the frame, two lines:
  PRACTICE BECOMES MEMORY
  MEMORY BECOMES SONG

COLOUR
Dominated by oxidized copper green and deep teal shadow. The only warm light
is amber ember glow from valve amplifiers and from the storm break behind the
colossus. Pale marble and bone highlights on skin and stone.

COMPOSITION
Keep every figure and all text within the central horizontal band of the
frame. The image will be cropped to a 2.5:1 letterbox.

DO NOT INCLUDE
No instrument held in anyone's hands. No science-fiction or humanoid robots.
No skulls, pentagrams, inverted crosses, or fire. No modern LED lighting rig,
truss, or lasers. No audience or crowd. No text other than that specified
above.
