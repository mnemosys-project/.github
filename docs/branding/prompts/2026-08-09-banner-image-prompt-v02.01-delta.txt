1. REPLACE THE WINGED CHERUBS WITH MECHANICAL BRONZE AUTOMATA.

   The small bronze figures at the left and right edges are currently winged
   cherubs. They are wrong. Replace them with mechanical automata.

   THEY MUST HAVE NO WINGS. Not feathered wings, not metal wings, no wings of
   any kind. They are not cherubs, angels, putti, or children.

   They are machines, and must plainly look like machines: bronze bodies built
   from riveted and overlapping plates, visible articulated joints at the
   shoulder, elbow, knee and knuckle, exposed hinges and pins, hands with
   segmented mechanical fingers, and smooth featureless faces or plain bronze
   masks with no human expression. Think of an ancient Greek clockwork
   servant, forged rather than born.

   There must be exactly THREE of them, and each must stand no more than half
   the height of the two women.

   Move them forward out of the corners and onto the wet stone stage floor,
   where the warm amber light from the amplifiers catches their polished
   bronze. Each one is working: the first coils a heavy cable, the second
   holds a sheet of music up to the light, the third adjusts a microphone
   stand.

   None of them stands on a plinth, touches an instrument, wears a laurel
   wreath, or resembles either of the two women in any way. They are clearly
   servants and stagehands.

2. RESTORE TWO MISSING INSTRUMENTS.

   Two instruments have become unrecognisable and must be rendered clearly, on
   stands among the others along the amplifier wall:

   - An AULOS: a matched pair of slender dark reed pipes, angled apart in a V
     from a single double mouthpiece, mounted upright on a stand.
   - A HURDY-GURDY: a wooden stringed instrument with a lute-shaped body, a
     wooden crank handle projecting from the tail end, and a small boxed row
     of wooden keys along the side of the neck.

   Both show green verdigris on their metal fittings, consistent with the
   other untouched instruments.

CHANGE NOTHING ELSE. Specifically, all of the following must survive exactly
as they appear now:

   - The two women: their faces, their living rain-soaked skin, their damp
     hair, their poses, their expressions, their size and their positions on
     their plinths. Do not make them larger, smaller, paler, or more like
     statues. They are the best thing in the image.
   - The camera position and the framing. Do not zoom in or pull back.
   - The colossal seated bronze statue and the water falling from her hands.
   - The ruins climbing the mountainside, the storm, the shafts of light, and
     the falling rain.
   - The hanging signboard, its wrought-iron bracket, and its two lines of
     text.
   - Every carved and lettered word, spelled exactly as it is now: MNEME,
     MELETE, AOEDE, MNEMOSYS, PRACTICE BECOMES MEMORY, MEMORY BECOMES SONG.
   - The blackletter lettering style and the Pegasus relief on the amplifier
     wall.
   - The amplifier wall, its amber pilot lights, and the cables running across
     the wet stone floor.
   - Every other instrument, including the kithara, the Flying V guitar, the
     electric bass, the theremin and the drum kit, in its current position and

   - The colour palette: oxidized green and deep teal, with amber the only
     warm light.
