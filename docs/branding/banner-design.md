## 1. Purpose

A banner image for the Mnemosys Project org profile — the full-bleed image at
the top of `profile/README.md`, in the position VERGIL and MIMIR already
occupy in their orgs.

The image is governed by `NAMING.md`. That document establishes that the
organization is named for memory and every tool under it is named for a Muse.
The banner's job is to render that scheme visible, so that a person arriving at
the org page absorbs the structure before reading a word of prose.

Two constraints came directly from the naming document and shaped everything
downstream:

1. **The three Muses are not peers.** Mneme is the organization and is
   explicitly *not* assigned to any tool. Melete is assigned. Aoede is
   reserved. A flat row of three sisters would contradict the document.
2. **Names must teach themselves.** Rule 6 requires recorded pronunciation
   because these are unfamiliar words meant to be spoken aloud. An image that
   labels its own roster does that work continuously and for free.

## 2. The House Style

Two sibling orgs already have banners, and they are identical by construction
because VERGIL and MIMIR are a deliberate benevolent/malevolent pair.

| Property | Both banners |
| --- | --- |
| Dimensions | 1983 × 793 — exactly 2.50:1 |
| Placement | Full-bleed, top of `profile/README.md`, `width="100%"` |
| Finish | Photorealistic cinematic render, heavy detail, deep background |
| Figures | One dominant foreground figure, left of centre, cropped at the chest |
| Depth | A city or landscape receding behind |
| Text | In-image signage carrying the thesis as an aphoristic couplet |
| Grade | One saturated hue across the whole frame — cyan/white vs. red/black |

VERGIL: *HUMANITY INSPIRES INTELLIGENCE / INTELLIGENCE ELEVATES HUMANITY.*
MIMIR answers it: *LOYALTY IS SAFETY / DISSENT IS TREASON.*

**What Mnemosys inherits:** the 2.50:1 frame, the photorealistic cinematic
finish, the in-image signage couplet, a single distinct hue identity.

**What Mnemosys discards:** the single-foreground-figure composition. That
grammar exists because VERGIL and MIMIR each had exactly one entity to depict.
It carries no meaning of its own and does not survive contact with a
three-figure hierarchy.

## 3. The Governing Idea

The image spans the ancient and the modern, and refuses to treat either as
decoration on the other. The sanctuary and the stage are the same structure.

This is the same discipline `NAMING.md` applies to names. That scheme works
because *Melete* actually means practice — the name is structurally true, not
merely evocative. Rule 4 makes the point explicitly: a Muse chosen only for
sounding pleasant weakens every other name in the set.

The visual equivalent is to reject anachronism-as-costume and use only
**hinges** — objects, gestures, and materials that are *authentically both*
ancient and modern, with no forcing required.

## 4. The Hinges

Each of these is genuinely old and genuinely current. None is a pun the image
has to sell.

| Hinge | Ancient | Modern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mano cornuta** | Mediterranean apotropaic sign against the evil eye, attested for millennia | Ronnie James Dio took it from his Italian grandmother; it now means heavy metal |
| **Doré chiaroscuro** | Engraved plates for Dante and Milton | The direct visual ancestor of metal album art |
| **The kithara** | The Muses' own instrument | Its silhouette is essentially a Flying V |
| **Amplifier cabinets** | Ashlar masonry, stacked plinths, altars | Backline |
| **Blackletter** | Medieval manuscript hand | Every metal logo ever drawn |
| **Pegasus** | Struck Hippocrene open on Helicon, where the Muses were worshipped | A winged horse over a storm is already an album cover |
| **Bronze automata** | Hephaestus's golden handmaidens, *Iliad* 18 | AI assistants |

The Doré line carries a second payload. Doré's canonical work illustrates
Dante, and Dante's guide is **Virgil** — a real lineage connection back to the
Vergil Project that costs the image nothing and requires no robot.

## 5. Composition

Selected from four candidates: pediment, depth-staged, frieze, and stage/altar.
**Stage/altar** won because it resolves the two governing constraints on
*separate axes*, so neither has to fight the other:

- **Hierarchy runs vertically and into depth.** Mneme is colossal, rear, above.
- **Equality runs laterally.** Melete and Aoede are mirrored at matched scale.

The rejected alternatives and why:

| Option | Rejected because |
| --- | --- |
| Pediment | Perfect symmetry reads static and churchy; least compatible with the metal thread |
| Depth-staged (house grammar) | Staging the daughters at different depths silently ranks them |
| Frieze | Even scale across 2.5:1 leaves no focal point, and shares nothing with the siblings |

## 6. The Three Figures

**Mneme — the source.** A colossal bronze cult statue at rear centre, heavily
oxidized, scaled to read as architecture rather than as a person. She holds no
instrument and does not perform. Water pours from her cupped hands and runs
down into the stage — Hippocrene, the spring on Helicon. The water is the only
element touching all three figures, which makes the descent structural rather
than implied. Her position encodes rule 5 of `NAMING.md`: the organization
keeps the memory name, and the Muses descend from it.

**Melete — practice.** Stage left, mid-exercise, in motion, visibly working
rather than performing. Her attributes all mean *repetition under constraint*:

- A music stand with a sheet showing staff notation **and** tablature — the
  literal output of the melete tool.
- A mechanical pyramid metronome, in bright polished bronze.
- A wax tablet and stylus. A wax tablet is written, smoothed flat, and written
  again on the same surface. It is the only classical object that natively
  means repetition under erasure, which is the project's entire subject.

**Aoede — song.** Stage right, mirrored to Melete at identical scale and eye
level, mid-note with her head back. She holds nothing; one hand throws the
mano cornuta.
Behind her, racked scrolls and handwritten set lists — repertoire, the thing
the reserved `aoede` tool is meant to maintain.

The daughters are deliberately equal despite their different status in
`NAMING.md`. Aoede is reserved rather than built, but an image that showed her
as unrealized would need regenerating the moment she shipped. Both names are
claimed, both tools are intended, and the banner should not require maintenance
on every release.

## 7. The World

Night on Mount Helicon under a breaking storm. The stage is a wall of amplifier
cabinets stacked like ashlar masonry — grille cloth reading as weathered stone,
corners shod in bronze. Pegasus is carved in relief into the cabinet face,
occupying the position a band logo would occupy; he struck Hippocrene open, so
he belongs in the masonry rather than flying loose in the frame. Cables run
across the stage like vines and roots. Laurel wreaths and studded leather over
the chitons.

Instruments stand on stands across the stage, spanning roughly 2,500 years with
none favoured: **kithara, aulos, hurdy-gurdy, Flying V, electric bass,
theremin**, plus a drum kit with bronze shells gone green. The kithara and the
Flying V sit adjacent so the silhouettes rhyme.

The instrument spread is a deliberate correction. Melete v1 generates *bass*
exercises, but its architecture holds that exercises are abstract and
instruments are configurations. A bass guitar in a Muse's hands would
permanently narrow the org to one instrument in exactly the way the design
avoids. No instrument is held by anyone.

## 8. The Automata — Where AI Sits

Small bronze automata attend the rig at both wings: coiling cable, holding a
sheet up to the light, adjusting a microphone. They are Hephaestus's golden
handmaidens from *Iliad* 18 — self-moving attendants with understanding and
speech, the attested ancient Greek intelligent machines.

They are the smallest figures in the frame. They are servants. Not one of them
is a Muse, and not one touches an instrument.

That placement is the whole statement, and it is precise: **Engineers and
musicians conceived and direct this project, AI builds it under their
direction — engineering-led, not AI-led — and AI is not in the product.**
Melete embeds no model and calls no inference endpoint. The automata build and
maintain the stage; the Muses make the music.

Choosing Hephaestus's automata over a modern robot also keeps the image clear
of VERGIL and MIMIR, whose banners are *about* AI. Mnemosys is not. Here the
machines are staff.

## 9. Typography

| Text | Treatment | Position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MNEME / MELETE / AOEDE | Roman capitals, cut into stone | The three plinths |
| MNEMOSYS | Heavy blackletter | Front amplifier grille, band-logo placement |
| PRACTICE BECOMES MEMORY / MEMORY BECOMES SONG | Weathered sign | Upper right, sibling signage position |

The typography is itself a hinge: carved Roman capitals for the ancient half,
blackletter for the modern half, where blackletter is simultaneously a
medieval manuscript hand and the universal metal logo convention.

The couplet is not a slogan. It is the pipeline, in order —
**Melete → Mneme → Aoede** — and it names all three domains without naming the
tools. It follows the VERGIL two-line form and states the retention thesis as a
process rather than an assertion.

## 10. Palette and the Material Rule

**Verdigris and ember.** Oxidized copper green and deep teal shadow dominate;
the only warm light is amber valve-amp glow and the storm break behind the
colossus. Pale marble and bone highlights.

The grade was chosen over a warmer bronze-and-storm alternative because
verdigris *is* bronze decaying, and decay is the project's actual subject.
`NAMING.md` records the thesis plainly: the domain is retention, and the hard
problem is decay, not acquisition.

That yields the rule the image is built on:

> **Bronze stays bright where it is handled and used. It goes green where it is
> neglected.**

Polished: the metronome, the automata's hands, the fretboards, the rim of the
spring. Oxidized: the colossus, the architecture, the untouched instruments.

This renders the retention thesis as surface chemistry rather than as symbolism.
Practice keeps the metal bright. Neglect turns it green. It is the single detail
most worth preserving through any future revision.

Colour identity across the three orgs stays distinct: VERGIL holds cold cyan on
white, MIMIR hot red on black, Mnemosys oxidized green on teal.

## 11. Negative Constraints

- No instrument held in anyone's hands — instrument-neutral by design (§7)
- No science-fiction or humanoid robots — would collide with VERGIL and MIMIR
- No skulls, pentagrams, inverted crosses, or fire — the metal comes from light,
  posture, material, and the backline, never from costume-shop props
- No modern LED rig, truss, or lasers
- No audience
- No text beyond §9

## 12. Production Notes

Target generator is **GPT Image via ChatGPT**, chosen because it is what
produced the VERGIL and MIMIR banners and because it is markedly the strongest
of the available options at rendering legible in-image text. The prompt is
written as long natural-language prose accordingly, with no weighting syntax or
flags.

**Aspect ratio.** ChatGPT does not offer 2.50:1 directly; landscape output is
3:2. Generate landscape and crop to 2.50:1, then scale to 1983 × 793 to match
the siblings. The prompt therefore instructs that all figures and all text stay
within the central horizontal band, so the letterbox crop costs nothing.

**Text volume.** The image specifies ten rendered words across three separate
placements. That is enough that a garbled character is likely — the VERGIL
banner has a visibly mangled sign already. Regenerate rather than accept a misspelled Muse name;
the names are the part doing the teaching work.

**Legibility at thumbnail size was explicitly deprioritized.** The optimization
target is the full-width view on the org profile page. Fine detail is welcome.

## 13. Deferred

**Lethe.** At the stage edge, Mneme's water could split — one channel running
clear over polished bronze, the other sinking into dry cracked ground where the
bronze is corroded through. Lethe beside Mnemosyne, forgetting set against
remembering: the truest single statement the image could make about the project.

Held back deliberately. The frame already carries a lot, and this is the detail
most likely to turn to mud. Revisit for v2 once the base image is proven.

## 14. Decisions

| # | Decision | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Two-tier hierarchy, not a flat triad | Mneme is the org, not a tool (`NAMING.md` §3, rule 5) |
| 2 | Melete and Aoede rendered as equals | Both names claimed, both tools intended; avoids regenerating per release |
| 3 | Match the house style rather than diverge | Third sibling org; format and finish carry the family, not composition |
| 4 | Ancient/modern blend via hinges only | Same discipline as the naming scheme — the thing must actually *be* both |
| 5 | Stage/altar composition | Hierarchy and equality resolved on separate axes |
| 6 | Verdigris and ember grade | Verdigris is decay, which is the thesis; distinct from both siblings |
| 7 | Bronze bright where handled, green where neglected | Retention thesis as material fact rather than symbol |
| 8 | No instrument held; six instruments across 2,500 years | Exercises are abstract, instruments are configurations |
| 9 | AI as Hephaestus's automata, at the wings, as servants | AI built the project and is not in the product |
| 10 | Names in-frame as carved Roman capitals | `NAMING.md` rule 6 — unfamiliar words that must be spoken |
| 11 | Couplet is the pipeline, not a slogan | Melete → Mneme → Aoede, in order |
| 12 | Lethe deferred to v2 | Frame density; highest risk of muddying |
| 13 | Automata rendered as mechanical machines, not animated bronze | Cannot be confused with cherubs or with Muses; the mechanical-servant reading is itself an ancient/modern hinge |
| 14 | One cold prompt, then at most one edit | Every delta damaged something unmentioned; every from-scratch run gained ground (§16) |

## 15. Sources

- **Pausanias**, *Description of Greece* 9.29.2 — the Boeotian triad of Mneme,
  Aoede, and Melete worshipped on Mount Helicon. The primary attestation for
  the elder tradition, and the basis of the whole naming scheme.
- **Hesiod**, *Theogony* 1–8, 77–79 — the Muses of Helicon; the canonical nine
  as daughters of Zeus and Mnemosyne.
- **Homer**, *Iliad* 18.417–421 — Hephaestus attended by golden handmaidens he
  forged himself, possessing understanding, voice, and strength. The classical
  warrant for the automata.
- **Hippocrene** — the spring on Helicon opened by a strike of Pegasus's hoof;
  sacred to the Muses. Sources include Hesiod, *Theogony* 6, and Ovid,
  *Metamorphoses* 5.256–268.
- **Mnemosyne and Lethe** — the paired springs of memory and forgetting, known
  from the Orphic gold tablets and from Pausanias 9.39 (the oracle of
  Trophonius). Basis for the deferred §13.
- **Gustave Doré** — engraved illustrations to Dante's *Divine Comedy* (1861–68)
  and Milton's *Paradise Lost* (1866). The chiaroscuro reference, and the
  ancestor of the metal album-art tradition.
- **Mano cornuta** — Mediterranean apotropaic gesture against the evil eye;
  introduced to metal performance by Ronnie James Dio, who attributed it to his
  Italian grandmother.
- [`NAMING.md`](../../NAMING.md) at the repository root — the authoritative
  naming convention. This document is subordinate to it.

### v01 — `mnemosys-project-main-image-v01.png`

Produced by `2026-08-09-banner-image-prompt.txt`. ChatGPT / GPT Image.

**Output was 1983 × 793 natively** — an exact match for VERGIL and MIMIR, with
no crop or rescale required. The §12 concern about 3:2 landscape output did not
materialise. Leave the central-band composition instruction in place anyway; it
costs nothing and guards the case where it does.

**Honored.** Composition D intact and legible. Mneme colossal and seated at
rear centre, backlit through the storm break, water falling from cupped hands.
Melete and Aoede genuinely mirrored — matched scale, eye level and plinth
height. The hierarchy-vertical / equality-lateral split worked exactly as
designed.

All ten words rendered with correct spelling. All seven hinges landed: Pegasus
in relief on the cabinet face, the mano cornuta unmistakable, kithara and
Flying V adjacent with the silhouette rhyme visible, blackletter MNEMOSYS on
the amp wall. All six instruments plus the drum kit present, none held.

**Invented unprompted, and better than what was specified.** Ruined temples and
terraces climbing Helicon behind the stage. Visible falling rain. A wooden
signboard hung from a wrought-iron bracket on chains, instead of the flat sign
the prompt described. Mneme seated rather than standing, which reads more
convincingly as a cult statue. All four are now written into the v02
from-scratch prompt so they survive.

**Dropped or wrong.**

| # | Miss | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Melete and Aoede rendered as pale marble statues rather than living women | **Structural.** Mneme is a statue *because* she is not a working figure; three statues collapses the distinction the whole design rests on |
| 2 | The bronze automata are too small and too dark to read | Structural. The AI statement in §8 does not communicate if the automata cannot be seen |
| 3 | Melete's wax tablet and stylus absent | Significant — the most on-thesis object in the design |
| 4 | Material rule only half-applied; drum kit oxidized, but kithara, bass and Flying V bright while untouched | Moderate. The bright/green logic is not legible |
| 5 | Melete's expression reads startled rather than absorbed | Minor |

**Lesson.** The generator reliably honors *nouns* — objects, placements, text,
materials named as things. It drops *predicates*: aliveness, relative
prominence, and rules stated once as principle rather than enumerated as
instances. Both structural misses are of that second kind. v02 therefore states
the material rule twice — once as the rule, once as an explicit list of which
surfaces are bright and which are green — and gives aliveness its own headed
paragraph rather than an adjective.

### v01.02 — `mnemosys-project-main-image-v01.02.png`

Produced by `…-prompt-v02-delta.txt`, applied to v01 in the live session.
Output again 1983 × 793.

**Fixed.** Automata visibility, completely. Material rule, cleanly — kithara,
Flying V and bass all verdigris, bright bronze reserved for automata hands and
the metronome. Wax tablet added, and better than specified: Melete holds the
stylus and is writing on it. Melete's expression corrected to absorbed.

**Half-fixed.** Aliveness. Melete reads as a living woman; Aoede still reads as

Both women shrank in frame and a wide empty band of stage floor opened across

it had. Quantities must be capped explicitly, and prominence must be stated as
a relation ("no more than half the height of the women") rather than as an
adjective.

### v02 — `mnemosys-project-main-image-v02.png`

Produced by `…-prompt-v02.txt` in a fresh session. Output 1983 × 793.

**The best of the three, and the from-scratch prompt beat the delta.** Recorded
as a finding: with this generator, re-specifying from cold outperformed
iterating on a good image. Delta edits fixed what they were pointed at and
silently damaged what they were not.

**Fixed, and transformative.** Both women are unmistakably alive — rain-soaked,
flushed, straining, with real human faces. Against Mneme's weathered bronze the
distinction is now immediate, which is what §6 depends on. Framing and figure
prominence recovered to v01 scale. Wax tablet present, material rule applied,
Melete absorbed in her sheet, Aoede's mano cornuta clear and high.

All four of v01's unprompted gifts survived being written in explicitly: ruins
climbing Helicon, heavy visible rain, the sign on its wrought-iron bracket, and
Mneme seated. Asking for a gift did not lose it.

**Regressed.** The automata became **winged putti** — bronze cherubs at the
extreme edges. Wings put them in the wrong mythological register entirely
(Baroque decorative sculpture rather than Homeric machines), and at the frame
edges they read as ornament rather than as crew. This actively misstates §8:
winged bronze children are not what "AI built the stage and is not in the
product" is supposed to look like. Neither prompt mentioned wings; the model
appears to default small bronze attendant figures on a classical stage to putti.

**Weakened.** The aulos and hurdy-gurdy are no longer clearly identifiable,
shortening the 2,500-year instrument span that §7 exists to establish.

### v02.02 — `mnemosys-project-main-image-v02.02.png`

Produced by `…-prompt-v02.01-delta.txt`, applied to v02. **Usable. Accepted as
the working banner unless v03 beats it.**

**Fixed.** No wings. Exactly three automata, forward on the stage, plainly
mechanical. The aulos returned and reads correctly as paired reed pipes. Both
women survived completely intact — same faces, same rain, same poses, same
scale. The two things the preservation list defended hardest are the two that
held, which suggests the list works when it is specific.

**Cost.** MELETE's carved name is hidden behind the kneeling automaton, and the
wax tablet with it. The hurdy-gurdy never returned and the theremin was lost.
Slight camera pullback again.

**The automata came back as mechanical mannequins** — riveted plates, exposed
joints, segmented fingers. This is not a generator failure: it is a precise
execution of the delta, which asked for exactly those things in exactly those
words. The vocabulary was wrong, not the render.

Homer's golden handmaidens are described as looking like living young women
made of metal. The Greek conception of an automaton is **animated sculpture,
not assembled machinery**, so mechanism vocabulary — joints, rivets, hinges —
recruits the artist's-mannequin prior instead. To get an ancient automaton, ask
for a bronze statue that is alive; to get a machine, ask for mechanism.

**Decided:** keep them mechanical. Unmistakably machines cannot be confused
with either cherubs or Muses, which is precisely the §8 statement, and the
C-3PO lineage is itself an ancient/modern hinge of the kind the design is built
on. Recorded as decision 13.

### Workflow finding

Every delta run damaged something it was not pointed at — framing in v01.02,
MELETE's lettering in v02.02 — three for three. Every from-scratch run gained
ground. The working rule adopted, and worth carrying to any future image work
in this org:

> **One cold prompt, then at most one edit. If the edit does not land it, stop
> editing and re-specify from scratch.**

Delta damage compounds and is invisible until you diff the images. Re-specifying
costs one paste and carries forward everything already learned.

### v02.03 — `mnemosys-project-main-image-v02.03.png`

A deliberately minimal delta against v02.02. The entire request was to restore
MELETE's name to her plinth — one word, one stone, phrased as "one very minor
change."

**It worked.** MELETE is legible again, and with the far-left automaton gone
her corner is cleaner than in any previous version.

**It cost two things.** MNEME's inscription washed out to illegibility on the
rock beneath the colossus, and the third automaton was deleted rather than
moved — the model solved the occlusion by removing the figure.

**This is the decisive evidence for decision 14.** The smallest, politest,
most narrowly scoped request in the entire project still re-rolled the frame
and silently dropped an inscription and a figure. There is no such thing as a
small edit with this generator; every delta is a full regeneration wearing a
small edit's clothing. Four deltas, four unrequested losses.

**It also changed the canvas.** Noted on archiving, 2026-08-10: v02.03 is
**1984 × 793**, one pixel wider than the 1983 × 793 that every other generation
in this project produced. Nothing asked for it and nothing reported it. A
one-pixel drift is harmless here because v02.03 was not accepted, but it is the
same failure mode as the dropped inscription — the delta changed something
outside its stated scope and said nothing. Check output dimensions after any
edit run.

**Both asks landed.** The drapery is dialed back to heavy opaque full-length

hair, faces and arms as instructed. The automata count corrected to three. All
four inscriptions, all seven instruments, metronome and wax tablet all held.

**It cost presence.** The camera pulled back and the composition flattened into
a wide tableau. Both women are smaller and pushed to the frame edges, Aoede
crowded by the right column with her inscription at the bottom margin, and the
three centre-stage automata now compete with the Muses for authority. The amber
light is flatter and cooler.

**Finding: framing has never been specified.** Every prompt in this project
described *what* is in the frame and never *how large* anything sits within it.
Figure prominence has therefore been luck in all seven images, and it swung
badly here. Any future image work in this org should state figure scale as a
requirement — how much of the frame height the primary figures occupy, and how
small secondary figures must stay relative to them.

### Carry these forward

Three findings from §16 apply to any future image work in this organization,
and none of them are specific to this banner:

1. **One cold prompt, then at most one edit.** Four deltas, four unrequested
   losses. If the edit does not land it, re-specify from scratch rather than
   editing again.
2. **The generator honours nouns and drops predicates** — and when forced to
   satisfy a predicate, it multiplies nouns instead. Told to make the automata
   visible, it added four more rather than lighting the two it had. Cap
   quantities explicitly; state prominence as a relation, not an adjective.
3. **Specify figure scale.** No prompt in this project ever stated how much of
   the frame a figure should occupy, so figure prominence was luck in all seven
   images — and it swung badly in v04, which was otherwise the most literally
   correct render.
